## 🚀 Introduction
Audio Converter for Windows brings audio conversion, extraction, and transcription together in one desktop program. As an audio file converter it moves your recordings from one format to another — including a direct audio converter to MP3 route — while its video to audio converter feature saves the sound from clips you already own. It also serves as an audio to text converter, running speech recognition on your machine so recordings become editable text. Since it's a Windows desktop app, the work is done locally rather than through a web service.

## ✨ Highlights

### Convert and extract
- **Format conversion** — Reshape recordings into the audio format you need, from MP3 to lossless and back.
- **Audio from video** — Treat it as a video to audio converter that separates the audio track from a video file on your PC.
- **Batch mode** — Process a group of files together to save time on large sets.

### Transcribe and organize
- **Speech-to-text** — Turn recordings into written text with on-device transcription.
- **Clear workflow** — Add a file, choose an output, and run — the layout keeps each step obvious.

1. Start Audio Converter and bring in an audio or video file from your computer.
